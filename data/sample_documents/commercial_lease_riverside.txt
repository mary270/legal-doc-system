COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Thi5 Commercia1 Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 12, 2022,
by and between:

LANDLORD: Harbor Properties LLC, a Delaware limited liability company,
with its principal office at 1400 Harbor Boulevard, Suite 800, Wilmington, DE 19801
("Landlord")

and

TENANT: Riverside Corporation, a New York corporation,
with its principal office at 500 Madison Avenue, Floor 22, New York, NY 10022
("Tenant")

WHEREAS, Landlord owns certain commercial real property located at
88 Commerce Drive, Newark, NJ 07102 (the "Premises"); and

WHEREAS, Tenant desires to lease the Premises from Landlord for commercial office use;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained,
the parties agree as follows:

1. LEASE TERM
   The lease term shall commence on April 1, 2022 ("Commencement Date") and
   shall expire on March 31, 2027 ("Expiration Date"), unless sooner terminated
   in accordance with the provisions hereof.

2. BASE RENT
   Tenant shall pay to Landlord base rent in the monthly amount of
   THIRTY-EIGHT THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED DOLLARS ($38,500.00), due on the
   first (1st) day of each calendar month, without demand, deduction, or offset.
   Rent for any partial month shall be prorated on a per diem basis.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
   Concurrently with the execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with
   Landlord the sum of SEVENTY-SEVEN THOUSAND DOLLARS ($77,000.00) as a
   security deposit (the "Security Deposit"), to be held by Landlord as security
   for the faithful performance by Tenant of all terms, covenants, and conditions.

4. PERMITTED USE
   The Premises shall be used and occupied solely for general commercial office
   purposes and for no other purpose without the prior written consent of Landlord.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
   5.1 Tenant's Obligations: Tenant shall, at its own cost and expense, maintain
   the interior of the Premises in good order and condition throughout the lease term.
   5.2 Landlord's Obligations: Landlord shall maintain the structural elements,
   roof, exterior walls, and building systems (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) in
   good working order, except for damage caused by Tenant's negligence.

6. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING
   Tenant shall not assign this Agreement or sublet the Premises or any part thereof
   without the prior written consent of Landlord, which consent shall not be
   unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed.

7. DEFAULT
   If Tenant fails to pay rent within five (5) days after the due date, or if Tenant
   otherwise defaults in the performance of any obligation under this Agreement and
   fails to cure such default within thirty (30) days after written notice from Landlord
   (or, if such default cannot reasonably be cured within thirty days, fails to
   commence cure within such period and diligently proceed to completion), then
   Landlord may terminate this Agreement and exercise all remedies available at law
   or in equity.

8. NOTICES
   All notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, overnight courier, or
   certified mail, return receipt requested, to the addresses set forth above
   or to such other address as a party may designate in writing.

9. GOVERNING LAW
   This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws
   of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict of law provisions.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
    This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect
    to the Premises and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, warranties,
    and understandings.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
